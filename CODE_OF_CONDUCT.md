# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can contribute without being harassed. Everyone, whatever their background or experience, is welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and in English so everyone can follow.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information
- Anything else a reasonable person would find hostile in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and ban people who break these rules. To report a problem, email the maintainers at the address listed in the repository profile. Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
